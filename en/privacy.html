Privacy policy

Draft for approval. Before publication the text is reviewed by a lawyer against the actual data-processing setup.

Who we are

Bali Property Desk is a property selection service in Bali. The data operator (legal entity and contact details for enquiries) — [to be confirmed before publication].

What data we receive

Your name, chosen messenger and phone number (WhatsApp, Telegram or Viber), email (optional) and your quiz answers. Technically — the traffic source (UTM tags, landing page address, referring site address) and the submission time. We do not ask for passport, payment or other sensitive data.

Why

To prepare a shortlist of options for your request, send it to you and contact you about this request.

Who the data may be shared with

If answering the request requires a suitable seller, developer or partner, we pass them your contact details and request parameters to the extent needed to get in touch. The request is delivered to the team via a server function into an internal work chat.

Analytics

The site runs an advertising platform pixel: it records a page visit and the fact that a request was sent. Your name, phone number and email are not passed to analytics.

What is stored in your browser

Only the quiz answers and traffic tags — for the duration of the session. Contact details are not stored in the browser.
