How foreigners can own property in Bali

Foreigners can't hold freehold title (Hak Milik) in Indonesia, but there are legal options. We'll suggest the one that fits your situation.

  1. 01

    Most common

    Leasehold — long-term lease

    Term set by the lease, with extension

    The right to use the property under a lease agreement for decades. Suits both living and renting out.

  2. 02

    In your own name

    Hak Pakai — right to use

    Up to 80 years (30 + 20 + 30)

    Registered to you personally if you hold an Indonesian residence permit (KITAS/KITAP). An option for your own residence.

  3. 03

    For rental and business

    PT PMA company — Hak Guna Bangunan

    Up to 80 years (30 + 20 + 30)

    Purchase through an Indonesian company with foreign capital. For those planning to rent out or run a business.

  4. 04

    If your spouse is Indonesian

    Through your spouse

    Perpetual ownership — full freehold (Hak Milik)

    The property is registered to your spouse, an Indonesian citizen. A marriage agreement on separate property is required.

We'll advise you in detail on your situation after your request.
