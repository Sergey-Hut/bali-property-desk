BALI PROPERTY DESK

Privacy policy

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Budi Castomo, an individual, who operates the Bali Property Desk property search service. Requests are received and processed by the controller. Send all data questions to privacy@balipropertydesk.com.

What data we collect

Your name, the messenger you want us to reply on (WhatsApp or Telegram), your phone number, and your Telegram username and email address if you provide them. Your questionnaire answers: property type, budget, areas, priorities, and timing. The request number. Technical data: advertising link tags (UTM), the address of the landing page, the address of the site you came from, the site language, the time the request was sent, your IP address, and your browser type. We do not ask for passport, payment, or other sensitive data.

How we use your data

To prepare a shortlist of options based on your request, send it to you, and contact you about your request.

Market analysis

The purpose is to find out whether there is real demand for property in Bali and which options interest people. To do this we review the questionnaire answers: property types, areas, budgets and requests. We look at aggregated figures (how many people chose each option), not names or phone numbers.

Mailings

We do not send marketing mailings and we do not add you to any mailing lists. The only reply you will receive is the reply to your request.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not pass your contact details to sellers, developers or other companies. The website and its technical setup are run by Sergey Ryzhov on behalf of the controller. The request is handled by services without which the website and request delivery cannot work: website hosting (GitHub Pages), the server function that receives requests (Vercel), Telegram, where the request is delivered, and the domain and DNS (Namecheap). These services operate in the United States and other countries, so your data may be processed outside Indonesia.

Analytics and cookies

The website uses the Yandex Metrica and Google Analytics 4 counters. They record visits and actions on pages (button clicks, completing the questionnaire, sending a request), and they set cookies and visitor identifiers. In Yandex Metrica, action recording on the website (Webvisor) and a click map are enabled. The counters are configured not to record what is typed into form fields. Your name, phone number and email are not passed to the counters.

Advertising pixel

When advertising starts, a pixel from the OpenAI advertising platform will appear on the website. It records page visits and the fact that a request was sent. Your name, phone number and email are not passed to the pixel.

What is stored in your browser

Questionnaire answers and advertising link tags are kept in the memory of your browser tab and disappear when you close the tab. The counters store their identifiers in cookies and in your browser's local storage: Yandex Metrica for up to one year, Google Analytics for up to two years. Contact details are not saved in your browser.

Consent

By sending a request, you agree to the processing of the data listed above to prepare the shortlist, contact you, and analyze the market. You can withdraw your consent by writing to privacy@balipropertydesk.com.

Retention periods

We keep requests and related data for 6 months from the date of the request, after which we delete them. The retention period for counter data is set by the counters' own settings, as described in the section "Analytics and cookies".

Age

The service is intended for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, do not send a request.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent. To do this, write to privacy@balipropertydesk.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in the country where you are located.
